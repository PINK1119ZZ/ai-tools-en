How to Choose Between DigitalOcean and Cloudways

Choose DigitalOcean if:

  • You have DevOps experience or a technical co-founder
  • You want maximum control over server configurations
  • You're optimizing for lowest possible cost
  • You need custom infrastructure (Kubernetes, custom networking)
  • You have time to handle server maintenance yourself

Choose Cloudways if:

  • You're non-technical or want to focus on product, not infrastructure
  • You need staging environments and team collaboration
  • You want 24/7 expert support via live chat
  • You're launching fast and can't spend weeks learning DevOps
  • You value peace of mind over saving $30/mo

The hybrid approach: Many founders start with Cloudways for speed, then migrate to DigitalOcean once they hit $10K+ MRR and can hire DevOps help. This lets you launch fast while keeping long-term costs low.

Common Mistakes SaaS Founders Make

Mistake #1: Over-engineering infrastructure before PMF

Don't build on AWS with Kubernetes and multi-region deployments when you have 10 users. Start with a $12/mo DigitalOcean Droplet. You can always migrate later — and you'll have revenue to fund it.

Mistake #2: Using 12 different marketing tools

Mailchimp + ConvertKit + ClickFunnels + Calendly + Teachable = $300/mo and integration hell. Systeme.io does 90% of this for $27/mo. Consolidate tools aggressively.

Mistake #3: Skipping product demo videos

Text descriptions convert at 2-3%. Videos convert at 4-6%. Spending 30 minutes in CapCut to make a demo video can double your signup rate.

Mistake #4: Treating all AI copywriting as final

ChatGPT gives you 80% of the way there — but the last 20% (brand voice, specific examples, emotional hooks) must be human-edited. Don't publish raw AI output.

Mistake #5: Ignoring security until after a breach

When you're working from coffee shops and airports, NordVPN isn't optional. One stolen laptop with unencrypted admin access can end your company.
