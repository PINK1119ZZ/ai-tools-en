The Weekly AI Workflow for Dropshippers

  1. Monday – Product Research (Claude Pro): Paste trending TikTok product lists and competitor catalogs. Ask Claude to identify gaps: high demand, low organic competition, margin above 40%.
  2. Monday – Copy Sprint (ChatGPT Plus): Write product descriptions, Facebook ad copy in three variants, and a five-email welcome sequence for the new product campaign.
  3. Tuesday – Creative Production (CapCut): Turn supplier footage into 5-10 short-form video ad variants using your proven template. Export for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.
  4. Wednesday – Funnel Setup (Systeme.io): Build a standalone product funnel for the top candidate. Set up abandoned cart sequence and post-purchase upsell.
  5. Thursday – Market Research (NordVPN + Claude): Check how competitors are positioning in your top two target markets. Identify pricing and messaging gaps.
  6. Friday – Data Review (DataCamp / Python): Run your weekly SKU margin report. Flag any products where ad spend exceeded 35% of revenue. Kill or pause them.
  7. Weekend – Digital Revenue (Gumroad): Package this week's learnings — a supplier research template, an ad script framework — and list it on Gumroad. Send a broadcast to your Systeme.io list.
