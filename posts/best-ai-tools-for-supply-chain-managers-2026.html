Most supply chain managers access this data over networks they don't control — hotel Wi-Fi, trade show floors, airport lounges.

NordVPN is the most audited, fastest, and most consistently reliable consumer VPN on the market. Its NordLynx protocol (built on WireGuard) delivers speeds that make it usable for video calls and real-time dashboard access, not just web browsing. The double VPN feature routes traffic through two servers — useful for compliance-sensitive markets. Threat Protection Pro blocks malicious supplier portal lookalikes and phishing domains that target procurement teams.

For teams: NordVPN Teams starts at $7/user/month and gives your entire procurement or logistics team centralized VPN management with dedicated IP options. For solo SCM professionals, the individual plan comes in at $2.99/month on a 2-year commitment.

The security argument is real: a single compromised supplier negotiation — where a competitor learns your target pricing before a contract renewal — can cost far more than years of VPN subscriptions.
