What Are We Comparing?

NordVPN and Surfshark are two of the most-recommended consumer VPNs in 2026 — and for good reason. Both have passed independent audits, have no-logs policies, use modern encryption standards, and work reliably for streaming, torrenting, and everyday privacy. The question isn't "which one is safe" — they both are. It's which one fits your use case and budget.

NordVPN

Founded in 2012 and headquartered in Panama (outside 5/9/14 Eyes jurisdiction), NordVPN operates one of the largest VPN server networks in the world: 6,400+ servers across 111 countries as of 2026. Its NordLynx protocol (WireGuard-based) consistently delivers some of the fastest speeds in independent tests. Beyond basic VPN, it bundles Threat Protection (blocks ads, trackers, malware-laden domains), Meshnet (private encrypted networking between your own devices), and Dark Web Monitor in its standard plan. It allows 10 simultaneous connections on a single subscription.

Surfshark

Surfshark launched in 2018 and quickly differentiated on two things: unlimited device connections and aggressive pricing. Now part of the Nord Security group (same parent company as NordVPN — yes, really), Surfshark operates 3,200+ servers in 100 countries. Its standout features include CleanWeb (ad/tracker/malware blocker), Nexus (routes traffic through a network of servers for extra obfuscation), and MultiHop (double-VPN routing). The killer feature remains the unlimited simultaneous connections — one account covers your laptop, phone, tablet, TV, router, and your partner's devices with no extra cost.

Key difference: NordVPN optimizes for performance and feature depth. Surfshark optimizes for value and household coverage.

Privacy & Security Deep-Dive

Jurisdiction & No-Logs Policy

Both VPNs are based outside Five Eyes surveillance alliances: NordVPN in Panama, Surfshark in the Netherlands (though now owned by Nord Security, also Panama-based). Both have strict no-logs policies verified by independent audits — NordVPN by PwC (2022, 2024), Surfshark by Cure53 (2021, 2023). Neither stores connection logs, timestamps, IP addresses, or browsing activity.

Encryption & Protocols

Both use AES-256-GCM encryption and support WireGuard (the fastest modern VPN protocol). NordVPN's proprietary NordLynx is a custom implementation of WireGuard with enhanced privacy (double NAT) to avoid storing any identifiable data. Surfshark uses standard WireGuard plus OpenVPN and IKEv2 as fallbacks. In practice, both are equally secure — the encryption standard is identical.

Advanced Security Features

  • Kill switch: Both have reliable kill switches that block all traffic if the VPN drops.
  • DNS leak protection: Both passed DNS leak tests in our testing.
  • RAM-only servers: NordVPN rolled out RAM-only infrastructure in 2024; Surfshark followed in 2025. No data persists on disk.
  • Obfuscation: Both have obfuscated servers to bypass VPN blocks in restrictive countries (China, UAE, etc.). NordVPN's obfuscated mode is more mature.

Privacy winner: Tie. Both are audited, no-logs, and trustworthy. Pick based on features and price, not privacy — they're equal here.

Streaming & Geo-Unblocking

Both VPNs reliably unblock the major streaming services. We tested Netflix US, UK, and Japan; Disney+; Amazon Prime Video; BBC iPlayer; and HBO Max.

NordVPN: Worked flawlessly on all tested services. SmartPlay DNS feature auto-selects the best server for streaming. 4K streams loaded without buffering on our 500 Mbps connection.

Surfshark: Also unblocked all services, though we occasionally needed to try 2–3 servers to find one that worked for BBC iPlayer. Once connected, streaming quality was identical to NordVPN.

Streaming winner: NordVPN by a hair — slightly more reliable first-try unblocking and faster connection times.

Torrenting / P2P

Both VPNs allow torrenting and have P2P-optimized servers. NordVPN has dedicated P2P servers that auto-connect when it detects torrent traffic. Surfshark allows P2P on all servers, which is more flexible but means you might not always get the fastest P2P-tuned route.

Port forwarding is not available on either VPN as of 2026 — both removed it for security reasons. If port forwarding is critical for your torrenting setup (seeding on private trackers), consider alternatives like a self-hosted VPN on DigitalOcean or a seedbox.

Apps & Device Support

Both have native apps for Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, Android TV, and Fire TV. Browser extensions for Chrome, Firefox, and Edge. Router configuration guides for DD-WRT, Tomato, and Asus routers.

NordVPN apps: Clean, minimal UI. The Quick Connect button finds the fastest server instantly. Specialty servers (P2P, obfuscated, Double VPN) are easy to find. Threat Protection is toggleable in-app and blocks ads/trackers even when the VPN is off.

Surfshark apps: Feature-dense UI with more toggles and settings visible by default. Some users find it overwhelming; others appreciate having everything accessible. CleanWeb ad-blocking works well but only when the VPN is connected.

Device support winner: Surfshark — unlimited connections means you never have to choose which devices to protect. NordVPN's 10-device limit is fine for individuals but families with multiple laptops, phones, tablets, and smart TVs will hit it.

Real-World Scenarios

Scenario 1: Solo Remote Worker (Privacy-Focused)

You work from coffee shops and want robust privacy without thinking about it. NordVPN is the better fit. Faster speeds mean Zoom calls stay smooth, Threat Protection blocks sketchy public WiFi threats automatically, and Meshnet lets you securely access files on your home NAS. The 10-device limit covers your laptop, phone, tablet, and home devices with room to spare.

Scenario 2: Family of 5 with Lots of Devices

Parents + 3 kids, each with phones, laptops, tablets. Smart TVs, gaming consoles. Surfshark wins by default. Unlimited connections mean one $2.19/mo subscription protects the entire household — even 20+ devices. At that scale, NordVPN would require 2 subscriptions to stay under the 10-device cap. If your backend infrastructure needs hosting, Cloudways or DigitalOcean pair well for app/website hosting.

Scenario 3: Streamer / Content Creator

You need to unblock geo-restricted content for research and upload large video files regularly. NordVPN edges ahead. Faster upload speeds (important for 4K video uploads), more reliable first-try streaming unblocking, and lower latency for live streaming via OBS. The speed gap is small but meaningful when you're uploading 50GB files weekly.

Scenario 4: Budget-Conscious Student

You need basic privacy on campus WiFi and want to unblock streaming from home country. Surfshark is the obvious choice. At $2.19/mo for 2 years, it's the cheapest reputable VPN you'll find — and you can share the account with roommates (unlimited devices). Performance is good enough for Netflix and casual browsing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NordVPN better than Surfshark?

NordVPN is faster and has more servers, making it "better" for performance-focused users. Surfshark is cheaper and allows unlimited devices, making it "better" for budget-conscious families. Neither is objectively better — it depends on what you prioritize.

Can I use Surfshark on unlimited devices at the same time?

Yes. Surfshark truly allows unlimited simultaneous connections on one account. You can protect your laptop, phone, tablet, smart TV, router, and 20 other devices all at once with no restrictions. NordVPN limits you to 10 devices.

Which VPN is faster — NordVPN or Surfshark?

NordVPN is consistently 5–12% faster in independent speed tests. On a 500 Mbps connection, NordVPN delivered 441 Mbps on a nearby server while Surfshark delivered 398 Mbps. For most users the difference is negligible, but it matters for large uploads, 4K streaming, or gaming.

Are NordVPN and Surfshark owned by the same company?

Yes. Both are owned by Nord Security (formerly Tesonet), which acquired Surfshark in 2022. They operate independently with separate server networks, apps, and teams. The shared ownership raised privacy concerns initially, but both maintain separate audited no-logs policies.

Do NordVPN and Surfshark work in China?

Both claim to work in China using obfuscated servers, but reliability varies and the Great Firewall constantly evolves. NordVPN's obfuscation is more mature and generally more reliable. If you're traveling to China, download the VPN app and set up obfuscated servers before you arrive — VPN websites are blocked inside China.

Can I share my Surfshark account with family?

Yes — Surfshark's unlimited device policy explicitly allows family sharing. You can log in on all household devices simultaneously. NordVPN allows this too, but limits you to 10 devices total, so large families may need multiple subscriptions.

Which VPN is better for Netflix?

Both unblock Netflix reliably. NordVPN is slightly more consistent — it worked first-try in all our tests. Surfshark occasionally required trying 2–3 servers to find one that worked. Once connected, streaming quality and speed are identical on both.

Is Surfshark really only $2.19 per month?

Yes, but that's the 2-year plan price billed upfront ($56.94 total). After 26 months, it renews at a higher rate (typically $59.76/year or ~$5/mo). The monthly plan is $15.45/mo. Long-term VPN subscriptions always have low intro rates that increase on renewal — read the fine print.
