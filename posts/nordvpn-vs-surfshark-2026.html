What Makes NordVPN Stand Out

NordLynx protocol — NordVPN built its own implementation of WireGuard with double NAT, solving WireGuard's privacy concern about storing IP addresses. The result is WireGuard speeds without the logging risk.

Threat Protection Pro — goes beyond a basic ad blocker. It scans downloads for malware, blocks trackers at the DNS level, and works even when you're not connected to a VPN server. It's one of the best built-in security features in any VPN.

Dedicated IP servers — if you need a static IP for whitelisting on client servers or corporate firewalls, NordVPN's dedicated IP add-on is clean and reliable. Surfshark has this too, but NordVPN's implementation is more stable.

Panama jurisdiction — this is a genuine advantage. Panama has no mandatory data retention laws and is outside 5/9/14 Eyes surveillance alliances. Surfshark's Netherlands base puts it technically inside 9 Eyes, though their no-logs policy has been independently verified.

Obfuscated servers — NordVPN maintains a dedicated fleet of obfuscated servers that disguise VPN traffic as regular HTTPS. Crucial for users in heavily censored regions like China or UAE.

What Makes Surfshark Stand Out

Unlimited simultaneous connections — this is Surfshark's killer feature. Install it on every phone, laptop, tablet, smart TV, and router in your household under a single subscription. NordVPN caps you at 10, which is plenty for individuals but tight for families.

CleanWeb 2.0 — Surfshark's ad and tracker blocker has been substantially upgraded. It now blocks cookie consent popups, first-party trackers, and works across all platforms including iOS, which many blockers struggle with.

Nexus routing technology — Surfshark routes traffic through their entire server network rather than a single server, making traffic analysis significantly harder. It's a genuine differentiator for privacy-minded users.

Full split tunneling on all platforms — unlike NordVPN, Surfshark supports split tunneling on macOS and iOS in addition to Windows and Android. If you're on a Mac and want to VPN only certain apps, Surfshark wins here.

Surfshark One bundle — for ~$2.69/mo on the 2-year plan, you get the VPN plus Surfshark Alert (data breach monitoring), Surfshark Search (private search), and Antivirus. It's a solid all-in-one deal.

Privacy & Security: The Real Differences

Both VPNs have passed independent no-logs audits and use RAM-only servers — meaning nothing persists after a reboot. But there are meaningful differences worth knowing:

Jurisdiction

NordVPN is headquartered in Panama, which has no mandatory data retention laws and sits outside 5/9/14 Eyes intelligence-sharing alliances. This is a genuine privacy advantage. Surfshark operates out of the Netherlands, technically inside the 9 Eyes alliance. That said, their Cure53 audit confirmed their no-logs policy holds up — so the practical risk is low either way.

Audit Credibility

NordVPN's 2024 audit was conducted by Deloitte, one of the Big Four accounting firms — adding significant credibility. Surfshark's Cure53 audit is well-respected in the security community. Both are solid; NordVPN's auditor carries more institutional weight.

DNS Leak Protection

Both VPNs run their own DNS servers and pass DNS leak tests consistently. Neither routes your DNS through third-party servers when the VPN is active.

⚠️ One Nuance on Surfshark

Surfshark was acquired by Nord Security (NordVPN's parent company) in 2022. They operate independently with separate infrastructure and teams, but they are now corporate siblings. For users who want completely independent providers, this is worth knowing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NordVPN faster than Surfshark in 2026?

NordVPN is marginally faster thanks to its NordLynx protocol, averaging 730–950 Mbps on a gigabit connection vs Surfshark's 680–890 Mbps. The difference is 5–8% — unnoticeable in real-world use on connections under 500 Mbps.

Which is cheaper: NordVPN or Surfshark?

Surfshark wins on price. Its 2-year Starter plan is ~$2.19/month vs NordVPN's ~$3.09/month. Over two years that's roughly $20–$40 in savings. Both offer 30-day money-back guarantees.

Does Surfshark have unlimited device connections?

Yes — Surfshark allows unlimited simultaneous device connections on a single account. NordVPN caps you at 10. For families or users with many devices, this is Surfshark's biggest practical advantage.

Which VPN is better for streaming in 2026?

Both unblock Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, and BBC iPlayer reliably. NordVPN has a slight edge accessing more Netflix regional libraries. Surfshark's unlimited devices make it better for households streaming on multiple screens at once.

Which VPN is more private: NordVPN or Surfshark?

NordVPN has a slight privacy edge due to its Panama jurisdiction (outside 5/9/14 Eyes) and Deloitte-audited no-logs policy. Surfshark (Netherlands, Cure53 audit) is still highly trustworthy — the practical privacy difference is minimal for most users.

Are NordVPN and Surfshark owned by the same company?

Technically yes — Surfshark was acquired by Nord Security (NordVPN's parent) in 2022. They operate independently with separate infrastructure, teams, and apps. But they are corporate siblings, which is worth knowing if you want fully independent providers.
