You're launching an online course and stuck between Teachable and Kajabi. Both are popular. Both have glowing testimonials. But one costs 3x more than the other.

Quick Verdict: Which Should You Pick?

Choose Teachable if:

  • You're starting your first course (budget <$100/mo)
  • You already use ConvertKit, MailChimp, or another email tool
  • You want the simplest course creation experience
  • You plan to sell mainly through affiliates or organic traffic

Choose Kajabi if:

  • You're building a full creator business ($10K+/mo revenue goal)
  • You need email marketing, funnels, and memberships in one place
  • You sell coaching, communities, or digital products alongside courses
  • You want high-converting templates and automation built in

Pricing Breakdown: The Real Cost

Teachable looks cheaper on paper, but transaction fees add up fast. Here's the real monthly cost at different revenue levels:

At $2,000/mo revenue:

  • Teachable Basic: $39 + $100 (5% fees) = $139/mo
  • Teachable Pro: $99 + $0 (0% fees) = $99/mo
  • Kajabi Basic: $119 + $0 (0% fees) = $119/mo

At $5,000/mo revenue:

  • Teachable Basic: $39 + $250 (5% fees) = $289/mo
  • Teachable Pro: $99 + $0 (0% fees) = $99/mo
  • Kajabi Basic: $119 + $0 (0% fees) = $119/mo

Takeaway: If you're making less than $1K/mo, Teachable Basic is cheapest. Above $2K/mo, Teachable Pro or Kajabi make more sense. Above $10K/mo, Kajabi's all-in-one suite saves you money on external tools.

Course Creation Experience

Teachable: Simple and Fast

Teachable's course builder is dead simple. Upload videos, add text lectures, insert quizzes. Drag to reorder. Done. You can have a full course live in under 2 hours.

The interface feels like Google Docs meets YouTube Studio. No learning curve. No confusion. Just content creation.

Best for: Coaches who want to ship fast, creators who record as they go, first-time course builders.

Kajabi: Powerful but Complex

Kajabi gives you way more control — custom lesson layouts, conditional content, advanced quizzes with branching logic. But it comes with a steeper learning curve.

The product library lets you bundle courses, memberships, and coaching packages. Great for scaling. Overwhelming for beginners.

Best for: Established creators building a full product suite, businesses with team members managing content.

Marketing and Sales: Where Kajabi Shines

This is where the price gap makes sense. Kajabi isn't just a course platform — it's a full marketing engine.

Email Marketing

Teachable: Basic email notifications when students enroll. No sequences, no broadcasts, no segmentation. You'll need ConvertKit ($29/mo), MailChimp, or ActiveCampaign ($29+/mo).

Kajabi: Full email automation built in. Sequences, broadcasts, A/B testing, tags, segments. It replaces ConvertKit entirely.

Cost savings: Kajabi's email tool alone saves you $30-50/mo compared to external platforms.

Landing Pages and Funnels

Teachable: Basic sales page builder. You'll likely need Carrd, Webflow, or Unbounce for high-converting landing pages.

Kajabi: 40+ proven funnel templates (webinar funnel, product launch, tripwire, etc.). Drag-drop builder. One-click upsells. It replaces ClickFunnels ($127/mo).

Cost savings: Another $50-130/mo saved if you were planning to use a funnel builder.

Kajabi

Integrations and Ecosystem

Teachable integrates with Zapier, ConvertKit, Mailchimp, Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Segment. You'll build your own stack.

Kajabi has native integrations for most tools, plus a full API. But since it's all-in-one, you need fewer integrations.

The trade-off: Teachable gives you flexibility. Kajabi gives you convenience.

When to Switch from Teachable to Kajabi

Start with Teachable if you're testing your first course. Consider switching to Kajabi when:

  1. You're hitting $5K+/mo in course revenue — Kajabi's cost becomes justified
  2. You're paying for ConvertKit + ClickFunnels + Circle — Kajabi consolidates all three
  3. You want to add coaching, memberships, or digital products — Kajabi's product suite scales better
  4. Student engagement is dropping — Kajabi's gamification and automation help

Migration: Plan for 1-2 weeks. Export Teachable content, rebuild in Kajabi, import student data via CSV. Not seamless, but doable.

Start with Teachable if: You're launching your first course, have a tight budget, or already use tools like ConvertKit and Circle. It's the fastest path to shipping.

Upgrade to Kajabi when: You're consistently hitting $5K+/mo, spending $100+/mo on marketing tools, or ready to build a full creator business with memberships and coaching.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Kajabi worth the higher price over Teachable?

Kajabi is worth it if you need an all-in-one platform with email marketing, landing pages, and funnels built in. If you only need course hosting and already have email/marketing tools, Teachable offers better value.

Which platform is better for beginners?

Teachable is more beginner-friendly with simpler setup, lower pricing ($39/mo vs $119/mo), and easier course creation. Kajabi has more features but steeper learning curve.

Can I migrate from Teachable to Kajabi later?

Yes, but it requires manual work. You'll need to export content from Teachable and rebuild in Kajabi. Student data can be exported via CSV. Plan for 1-2 weeks of migration time.

Which has better payment processing?

Both integrate with Stripe and PayPal. Kajabi has built-in payment processing with lower fees (2.9% + $0.30). Teachable charges 5% transaction fee on Basic plan, 0% on Pro+ plans.

Does Kajabi include email marketing?

Yes, Kajabi includes full email marketing automation, sequences, and broadcasts. Teachable only has basic email notifications — you'll need external tools like ConvertKit or MailChimp.

Which platform has better course completion rates?

Kajabi reports 15-20% higher completion rates due to better gamification, progress tracking, and automated engagement sequences. Teachable focuses on simplicity over engagement features.

Can I use my own domain on both platforms?

Yes, both support custom domains. Teachable requires Pro plan ($99/mo) for custom domain on courses. Kajabi includes custom domains on all plans starting at $119/mo.

Which is better for selling coaching alongside courses?

Kajabi wins for coaching with built-in scheduling, memberships, and community features. Teachable requires third-party integrations like Calendly or Circle for coaching add-ons.

Do I need hosting for Teachable or Kajabi?

No, both are fully hosted SaaS platforms. All infrastructure, security, and scaling is handled. You only need a domain name if you want custom branding.
