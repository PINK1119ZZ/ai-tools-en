My Personal AI Blogging Workflow (Step-by-Step)

This workflow produces 2-3 publish-ready blog posts per week, running 6-8 hours total per week:

Monday: Planning & Research (90 minutes)

  1. Keyword research (30 min): Use ChatGPT to analyze Search Console data, identify content gaps, generate 10-15 keyword ideas per topic
  2. Competitor analysis (30 min): Use Claude to analyze top 5 ranking articles for target keyword, synthesize gaps and opportunities
  3. Content calendar (30 min): Use ChatGPT to create outlines for 3-4 posts, schedule in editorial calendar

Tuesday-Thursday: Content Creation (3-4 hours total)

  1. Draft with ChatGPT (20 min per post): Provide outline + target keywords, generate 1500-2000 word first draft
  2. Refine with Claude (25 min per post): Paste ChatGPT draft, ask Claude to improve flow, add depth, insert examples, refine for natural voice
  3. Human editing (15 min per post): Add personal insights, update examples, verify facts, adjust tone
  4. Optimize (10 min per post): Meta description, alt text, internal links, FAQ schema

Friday: Video & Distribution (2 hours)

  1. Create video version (30-40 min per post): Screen recording or stock footage + CapCut editing with auto-captions
  2. Email newsletter (20 min): Use ChatGPT to create email summarizing week's posts, schedule in Systeme.io
  3. Social promotion (20 min): Use ChatGPT to generate platform-specific captions for LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook
  4. Pinterest (20 min): Create pins + video pins in CapCut, schedule

Monthly: Analysis & Optimization (2-3 hours)

  1. Performance review: Use DataCamp scripts to analyze top/bottom performers, identify update opportunities
  2. Content updates: Use Claude to refresh 2-3 older posts with new data, improved depth
  3. Strategy adjustment: Review keyword rankings, traffic trends, adjust content calendar based on data

Result: 12-15 high-quality blog posts per month, plus supplementary video content and consistent email communication—all in under 30 hours/month total.
