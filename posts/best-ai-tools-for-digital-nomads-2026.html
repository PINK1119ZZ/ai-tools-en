NordVPN
Best for Security on Public Wi-Fi
9.8 / 10

For digital nomads, NordVPN is not optional — it's infrastructure. Every coworking space, café, and airport lounge runs unsecured or semi-secured Wi-Fi. Without a VPN, your client credentials, banking sessions, and project files are visible to anyone on the same network running a packet sniffer.

Beyond security, NordVPN gives nomads three superpowers: geo-testing client sites from 59+ countries without changing location, accessing streaming services and tools blocked in certain countries, and routing around payment processor flags that trigger when your IP switches from Portugal to Thailand mid-subscription.

The Threat Protection Pro feature blocks trackers and malware-serving ads at the DNS level — meaningfully reducing the risk of drive-by infections on sketchy hotel Wi-Fi. The kill switch ensures your traffic stops completely if the VPN drops, preventing accidental data exposure.

  • 6 simultaneous connections — laptop, phone, tablet, all covered
  • 59+ countries — test geo-restricted client deliverables anywhere
  • No-logs policy — independently audited by Deloitte
  • Meshnet — connect to your home server or team devices securely
  • ~$3-5/mo on a 2-year plan — cheapest insurance you'll buy

✅ Pros

  • Essential security layer on any public network

  • Blocks IP-based payment processor flags

  • Fast NordLynx protocol (WireGuard-based)

❌ Cons
