Frequently Asked Questions

What AI tools do professional event planners actually use in 2026?

The most common tools among professional event planners in 2026 are CapCut for promo and recap videos, Systeme.io or similar platforms for automated registration and email, and cloud hosting (Cloudways or DigitalOcean) for their event websites. Many are also using AI writing tools for RFP responses, venue descriptions, and social media captions.

Can I use AI tools to automate event registration and ticketing?

Yes. Systeme.io handles registration page creation, email confirmation automation, reminder sequences, and even payment collection. For dedicated ticketing with seat maps, you'd pair it with a ticketing-specific tool, but for webinars, workshops, and smaller events, Systeme.io handles the complete flow.

What's the best free AI tool for event planners just starting out?

CapCut and Systeme.io are both genuinely free with no credit card required for their core features. CapCut's free tier handles most event promo video needs, while Systeme.io's free plan covers up to 2,000 contacts and 3 funnels — enough for most early-stage event businesses.

How do event planners use AI for social media marketing?

The most effective approach is using CapCut's AI features to create short-form video content — event teasers, speaker spotlights, venue reveals, and countdown posts. AI writing tools handle captions and hashtag strategies. Systeme.io's email sequences then convert social engagement into registrations.

What hosting do I need for a high-traffic event registration page?

If you expect traffic spikes (ticket announcements, influencer promotions), DigitalOcean or Cloudways are the right choices. They handle autoscaling and CDN caching so your registration page stays fast when hundreds of people hit it simultaneously. Budget shared hosting will fail under that load.

Can event planners make passive income using these tools?

Absolutely. Gumroad lets you sell event planning templates, run-of-show documents, budget calculators, vendor contact lists, or full courses with zero monthly fees. Systeme.io handles the email marketing funnel that drives those sales. Many experienced event planners earn $500–$2,000/mo in passive template income.

Is NordVPN necessary for event planners?

It's strongly recommended for anyone who regularly works on public networks — venue Wi-Fi, hotel connections, conference center internet. These are frequent targets for data interception. Client contracts, attendee data, and financial information are all sensitive enough to warrant the ~$4/mo investment in encryption.

What AI tools help with post-event reporting and client deliverables?

DataCamp skills in data visualization and spreadsheet automation significantly improve the quality of post-event reports. For the reports themselves, AI writing tools can turn raw data into polished narratives. CapCut handles the video recap. Systeme.io can automate the post-event survey collection that feeds the data.

How do I choose between DigitalOcean and Cloudways for my event site?

If you're building on WordPress (the most common setup for event planner websites), go with Cloudways — it's managed WordPress on top of cloud infrastructure, no server administration needed. If you need custom apps, APIs, or non-WordPress setups, DigitalOcean gives you more control. Many event agencies use both: Cloudways for WordPress client sites, DigitalOcean for custom applications.
