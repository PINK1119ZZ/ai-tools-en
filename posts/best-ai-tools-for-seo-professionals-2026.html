The Ultimate SEO AI Workflow (Step-by-Step)

Here's how I combine these 9 tools into one efficient SEO workflow that saves 15-20 hours/week:

Step 1: Keyword Research & Content Strategy (ChatGPT)

  • Paste keyword list into ChatGPT → cluster by search intent
  • Analyze top 10 SERP competitors for target keyword
  • Generate content brief: H1, H2s, word count, FAQs, schema markup
  • Create 50 meta descriptions for existing content (3 minutes)
  • Time saved: 1.5 hours → 15 minutes

Step 2: Technical SEO Audit (Claude)

  • Export Screaming Frog crawl (CSV or Excel)
  • Upload to Claude → ask for orphaned pages, redirect chains, schema errors
  • Generate bulk .htaccess redirects (regex patterns included)
  • Audit Core Web Vitals issues from PageSpeed Insights JSON
  • Time saved: 3 hours → 45 minutes

Step 3: Core Web Vitals Optimization (Cloudways)

  • Migrate site to Cloudways (if on shared hosting)
  • Enable Redis caching + Cloudflare CDN
  • Test page speed on staging before pushing live
  • Monitor LCP, CLS, FID in Google Search Console
  • Impact: LCP 3.2s → 0.9s, position 8 → position 3

Step 4: Video Content for SEO (CapCut)

  • Record screen demo or talking-head explainer video
  • Auto-generate captions in CapCut (15+ languages)
  • Export transcript → paste on webpage for on-page SEO
  • Embed video in blog post → markup with VideoObject schema
  • Impact: Ranks #1 for "how to" keywords with embedded video

Step 5: Lead Generation Funnel (Systeme.io)

  • Create "Free SEO Audit" landing page (Systeme.io builder)
  • Automated email sequence: Day 1 audit report (ChatGPT-generated), Day 3 case study, Day 7 consultation CTA

Step 6: Monetize Your SEO Expertise (Gumroad)

  • Package your best ChatGPT prompts for SEO ($27-47)
  • Sell technical audit checklists ($97)

  • Income: $2,400/month passive revenue

Step 7: Competitor Research (NordVPN)
