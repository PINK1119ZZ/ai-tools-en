9.4/10
🔬 Best Security Lab & Research Infrastructure

Spin up isolated Droplets for pentesting labs, honeypots, SIEM deployments, and vulnerability research in minutes. Developer-friendly, transparent pricing, and solid network isolation.

Security researchers need disposable, isolated infrastructure. Spinning up a VM on your local machine leaves artifacts and risks cross-contamination. DigitalOcean Droplets solve this: you get a clean Linux instance in a specific datacenter region, fully isolated, with fine-grained firewall control, in about 55 seconds.

Common use cases: deploying a Wazuh or Elastic SIEM instance for a client POC without touching their environment; running honeypots to observe attack patterns; setting up Kali Linux Droplets for remote pentest teams; or hosting an internal Vault instance for secrets management. DigitalOcean's VPC networking lets you group Droplets into private networks with no public internet exposure.

For security consultants who track infrastructure costs carefully, DigitalOcean's per-hour billing means a full research lab costs $5–15 for a weekend engagement, then disappears. No sprawling monthly commitments.
