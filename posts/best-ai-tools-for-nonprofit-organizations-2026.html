7. NordVPN — Best Security for Remote Nonprofit Staff

Nonprofits handle sensitive data: donor financial information, beneficiary records, grant application details, staff communications. Remote workers accessing this data from home networks or coffee shops create security vulnerabilities that could result in a breach — and a breach can devastate donor trust.

NordVPN protects every connection with military-grade encryption, blocks malicious sites with Threat Protection, and provides a kill switch that cuts internet access if the VPN drops — ensuring donor data is never exposed. At ~$3.99/mo per device on an annual plan, it is one of the lowest-cost security investments available.

For nonprofits operating in countries with internet restrictions or doing international fieldwork, NordVPN also unblocks restricted platforms, ensuring teams can communicate and access resources regardless of location. Teams plans allow centralized management across all staff devices. See the full NordVPN review for a detailed breakdown.

✅ Pros

❌ Cons

What is the best free AI video tool for nonprofit storytelling?

How can nonprofits use Gumroad to generate revenue?

Nonprofits can use Gumroad to sell digital products like e-books, impact reports, training guides, and event tickets. With no monthly fee and a transparent transaction model, it is one of the lowest-risk ways to add a revenue stream alongside donations.

Is DataCamp useful for nonprofit staff training?
